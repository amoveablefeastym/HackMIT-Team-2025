**Subjective:**  
Alex reports feeling tired and experiencing poor sleep, waking multiple times at night and feeling sluggish during the day. He notes increased work stress, mild depression, and anxiety (rated 6/10). He has lost a couple of pounds due to skipped meals and lacks motivation for exercise.

**Objective:**  
No physical examination was conducted. Alex's current medications include occasional over-the-counter melatonin.

**Assessment:**  
Stress-related sleep disturbance, mild anxiety, and depressive symptoms.

**Plan:**  
1. Improve sleep hygiene: No screens 30 minutes before bed, consistent sleep schedule, limit caffeine after noon.  
2. Stress management: Journaling or creating a "worry list."  
3. Exercise: Start with 15 minutes of light movement three times a week.  
4. Referral to cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I).  
5. Follow up in 4 weeks; send sleep diary template and resources through the patient portal.
